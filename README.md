### 🎯 About me

I'm a quantitative developer focused on the intersection of **financial
mathematics** and **high-performance software engineering**. I build trading
infrastructure and pricing/analytics tooling from first principles — event-driven
backtesters, derivatives pricers, statistical-arbitrage research pipelines, and
low-latency matching engines. I'm currently applying to **Master's programs in
Quantitative Development / Financial Engineering**.

- 🔭 I care about **correctness first, then speed**: everything I ship is tested
  and cross-validated.
- 🧮 Comfortable across **stochastic calculus, time-series econometrics,

  behaviour).
- 🛠️ I like turning textbook models into clean, reproducible, benchmarked code.

### 🚀 Featured projects

| Project | Language | What it does |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| [**Event-Driven Backtesting Engine**](https://github.com/Patelmeet21/backtesting-engine) | Python | Modular event-loop backtester (data → strategy → portfolio → execution) with momentum & mean-reversion strategies and a full risk-metrics suite (Sharpe, Sortino, drawdown, Calmar). |
